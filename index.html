Introduction

In today’s digital age, social media is deeply embedded in the daily lives of college students. While it offers benefits such as staying connected with others and sharing personal experiences, there are growing concerns about its impact on mental health, particularly in relation to increased stress and anxiety. This project explores the research question: How does social media use impact college students’ mental health? We hypothesize that while social media can have both positive and negative effects, excessive use is more likely to be detrimental than beneficial. To investigate this, we conducted extensive research on existing studies and collected primary data through a survey of current college students regarding their social media habits. The purpose of this project is not only to raise awareness about how social media may be affecting students, but also to encourage more mindful usage and stronger self-regulation when it comes to daily scrolling.
